Get Involved

Find your role in the planetary health movement

Whether you're a student, educator, professional, or simply passionate about the future of our planet—there's a place for you here.

Choose your path below and we'll help you get started.

Become a Member

Join a growing global community of youth dedicated to planetary health. Get access to resources, events, and a network of changemakers.

Start a Club

Bring planetary health to your university or high school. We'll provide toolkits, guidance, and connection to our global network.

Become a Youth Ambassador

Represent planetary health in your region. Help expand our reach, organize local events, and connect communities to the global movement.

Join a Training

Build your skills through workshops, webinars, and capacity-building sessions. Let us know what you're interested in learning.

Partner With Us

We collaborate with organizations, institutions, and networks that share our vision. Let's explore how we can work together.

Prefer email? Reach us directly at hello@planetaryhealthyouth.org
