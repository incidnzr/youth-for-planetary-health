Collective Accomplishments

What our members have achieved together

UN Representation

Represented at the 2026 ECOSOC Youth Forum at UN Headquarters in New York City, advocating for planetary health in global policy.

50+ Clubs Established

Planetary health clubs launched in high schools and universities across 30+ countries, from the US to Kenya to Indonesia.

Community Projects

Over 100 youth-led projects including native plant installations, sustainable swaps, urban farming, and awareness campaigns.

Global Network

Built a diverse network of 500+ members, mentors, and HeForShe advocates across multiple continents and cultures.

Media & Outreach

Educational reels, podcasts, infographics, and student interviews reaching thousands of young people worldwide.

Partnerships

Collaborations with the Planetary Health Alliance, Ecopsychepedia, Climate Junction, and local governments.
