What We Do & Our Impact

Five pillars driving planetary health forward

1. Learn & Share

We raise awareness and understanding through talks, campaigns, teach-ins, webinars, and accessible learning resources that make planetary health understandable and relevant.

Impact Goals

  • Reach 100,000 people educating about planetary health in the next two years
  • Engage new audiences across underrepresented regions

2. Train & Build Capacity

We organize training sessions, skill-building workshops, mentorship opportunities, and project incubators to strengthen youth leadership in planetary health.

Impact Goals

  • Develop a global pipeline of youth planetary health leaders
  • Launch project incubators across multiple regions

3. Lead & Act

We design and implement active leadership opportunities and youth-led initiatives in the planetary health field to emphasize the connection between planet's health and human's well-being, while encouraging action toward a sustainable future.

Impact Goals

  • Launch youth-led projects across multiple regions
  • Establish a global working group on eco-anxiety to address mental health and climate distress

4. Clubs & Systems

We support planetary health clubs within high schools and universities, collaborating with educators and communities to integrate planetary health into school life and local decision-making.

Impact Goals

  • Build a global network of planetary health clubs
  • Integrate planetary health into formal and informal education systems

5. Grow the Network

We mobilize youth ambassadors and partners to expand Planetary Health Alliance (PHA) membership across regions, especially the countries where no PHA members exist.

Impact Goals

  • Involve non-profit organizations from 30 new countries in the planetary health movement and the Planetary Health Alliance
